This revision changes the scene from mostly independent wire volumes into a connected, layered field:

- Five shape grammars: sheared meridians, cross-sectional hoops, braided loops, crossing double lobes, and corrugated saddle loops.
- 24–36 primary curves per volume, plus independently offset, rotating secondary structures inside the 15 large volumes.
- Nine seven-strand branching links connect selected moving centers. Their bends and separation evolve independently; they increase large-scale interaction rather than only adding density inside each object.
- Group travel increases to approximately 66–94 pixels horizontally plus a 24-pixel secondary component; vertical travel and transient displacement also increase.
- Independent axis rotation rises to .72–1.25 radians/second; pitch and contour flexing increase. Traveling spatial waves deform the wires themselves instead of relying only on rotation.
- Existing bass/mid/high/RMS/centroid mappings and decaying transient response remain. Spatially delayed audio lookup and autonomous motion continue throughout all three smoothly interpolated states.
