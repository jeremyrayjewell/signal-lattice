## Independent implementation

Scene R tiles an 8x5 grid of 120px square cells across the 960x540 frame on black. Each cell independently fixes a base 90-degree rotation, mirror, two comb colors, two teeth counts, and whether it carries an arc accent (seeded once). The two comb shapes are generated by an independently written zigzag function (index-based alternation between an edge coordinate and a linearly tapered diagonal), not the source's vertex loop, anchored at opposite corners as in the reference. Flat opaque fills with simple draw-order occlusion; no additive compositing, matching the source's flat character.

## Motion and music

- Autonomous: each comb's taper depth breathes gently; the two combs in a cell slowly counter-rotate against each other (a slow scissoring motion); arc-accent cells rotate their arc continuously.
- A small delay offset keyed to each cell's grid position (`(col+row) * 0.018s`), reused from Scene P, sends transients rippling diagonally across the grid.
- Bass: taper depth (spike length) swells.
- Mids: comb-vs-comb scissoring speed increases.
- Highs: arc rotation speed increases.
- Transient impulse: brief per-cell lightness flash, riding the ripple delay.
- RMS: modest overall lightness boost.
- Centroid: subtle hue bias on comb fills.
- A slowly traveling subset of cells (the same per-id sine "selected" technique used in Scenes N/O/P) reads with slightly boosted lightness.

State interpolation reuses the established calm 0-5s, transition to active 5-11s, transition to extreme 11-20s, extreme hold 20-25s schedule. Motion .45/.8/1.15, articulation .45/.8/1.2, impulse .4/.9/1.4, detail .45/.75/1 (articulation/detail reserved for future refinement). Existing feature smoothing/decay is unchanged: fast 25/160ms, slow 350/1100ms, bass 120/850ms, high residue 10/500ms, transients 650ms.
